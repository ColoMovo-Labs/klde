# Image generation brief

Mode: built-in image generation; original logo, then targeted edit.
Transparent background requested for both. Final file: `owode-logo.png`.

## Original generation

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final original desktop-environment product icon for owo Desktop Environment (owoDE), to replace its crude outlined fox face. Create ONE centered standalone symbol on a truly transparent background, square canvas. The mark should suggest the lowercase expression 'owo' through two bold soft open rings as eyes joined by a small flowing w-shaped smile; tiny organic fox-ear cues may emerge naturally from the outer silhouette. Elegant professional open-source operating system identity with tasteful playful kemono warmth, Material Expressive-inspired geometry and WinUI-quality optical clarity. Strong simple silhouette, balanced negative space, legible at 24 and 48 pixels, generous but not excessive outer breathing room. A cohesive blue-to-periwinkle-to-soft-cyan gradient within the symbol, limited to 2 or 3 broad solid shapes, clean precise edges, flat vector-like graphic, no thin outlines. No badge background, no enclosing tile, no lettering, no text, no mockup, no watermarks, no shadow outside the symbol, no fur detail, no existing brand likeness. Deliver just the one large finished icon, transparent around and through its negative spaces.

## Selected revision

Edit the just-generated owo fox logo into a crisp production app icon. Preserve its recognizable two circular 'o' eye rings, small w smile, and two ears, but REMOVE ALL glow, haze, light rays, shadows, airbrushing, translucent filled eye disks and background color. The finished symbol must be fully opaque with clean precise edges and genuinely transparent negative spaces: hollow circles for the eyes, transparent inside each o, transparent outside. Use only three broad smooth simple geometric shapes, blue/periwinkle/cyan, a very restrained internal gradient. Flat vector-like minimalist logo, optical clarity at 24px, no illustration detail. Square canvas, one centered symbol occupying 80% width, no letters, no labels, no mockups.
