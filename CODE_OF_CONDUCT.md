# Community conduct

KLDE welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, identities, abilities and
experience. Critique the work with evidence; treat the person with respect.
Ask before reusing personal information or moving a private conversation into public.

We expect constructive review, acknowledgement of mistakes and respect for a
person's decision to leave a discussion. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse,
sexual attention without consent, doxxing and deliberate disruption are unacceptable.
This applies to repository discussions, issues, reviews and project-controlled spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, pause a discussion, warn a
participant, or restrict participation according to severity and repeated behavior.
Actions should be proportionate, explained when safe and documented privately
without exposing a reporter. A maintainer involved in a complaint should recuse
themself; another uninvolved organization owner should review any appeal.

Do not publish a conduct report with sensitive details in an issue. Contact an
uninvolved maintainer through a contact method they have explicitly made public.
A dedicated private reporting contact has not been established yet. Establishing
and verifying it is an administrative launch task; no monitored inbox is implied.
If no appropriate contact is available, GitHub's own abuse-reporting controls remain
available. We do not promise private handling through a public issue thread.
