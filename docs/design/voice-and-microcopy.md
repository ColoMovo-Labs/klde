# Voice and microcopy

Standard is warm, brief, calm and clear. Cute adds sparse affection, a light
Kemono hint and occasional emoticons, not baby talk or an emoji on every label.
English and Chinese are authored intentionally; neither is a literal afterthought.

Examples: Standard “Your little controls.” / “快捷控制”; Cute “A few little
switches for you~” / “小开关都在这里呀～”. Ambient weather may be shy; a privacy
permission cannot be coy. Security, legal, authentication, destructive operations,
storage failures, certificates and critical faults always use professional,
unambiguous Standard messages. A voice pack cannot override those namespaces.

Keep action labels and consequences clear. A shutdown preview must state that it
is simulated; actual destructive confirmation must identify the operation and
consequence. Never turn disk failure into a joke or hide permission disclosure.

Catalog IDs describe meaning, not English spelling. Cute overrides require a
Standard base and fall back automatically. Review placeholders, truncation,
accessible names and both locales. The prototype provides a few selected Cute
strings rather than duplicate catalogs. Locale and voice are session preferences
and never invented system locale names.
