# Visual Lab reference study

The two supplied 17 October screenshots are first-class composition references,
not assets to redistribute. Their user identity, messages, proprietary wallpaper
and application marks stay out of KLDE fixtures and published screenshots.

Extracted behavior: separated top islands with wallpaper gaps; glanceable CPU,
memory, sensors and battery; expandable status overflow; workspace/date/time as a
stable central anchor; a context capsule; a large optional clock; and a left
information/notification/weather panel with nested, clearly weighted cards.

KLDE translates this into a configurable module registry and adaptive sections,
Comfortable/Balanced/Dense metrics, a tabbed notification center and an optional
ambient widget layout. Density expresses confidence rather than noise. Normal,
warning, critical and unavailable are distinct, with text/shape alongside color.

Keep the earlier original snow mountains, round weather, floating dock, soft
surfaces and restrained Kemono voice. Add original abstract/quiet/subtle wallpapers
and mocked accent palettes. Dark automatic accent returns to soft teal. Shape
hierarchy mixes discs, compact pills, expanded active workspaces, strong scene
cards and thick interaction tracks. Reference pixel geometry is not copied.
